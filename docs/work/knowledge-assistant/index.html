Enterprise Operations Knowledge Assistant

A RAG proof of concept for finding workflow instructions and troubleshooting guidance inside SOPs and runbooks.

Project context

Résumé-based project summary
Updated October 2026

Tools & concepts

RAGLlamaIndexLLMOperational documents

Workflow diagrams illustrate the documented approach, rather than a deployed system.

The problem

Operational guidance often sits across process documents and runbooks. Finding the relevant instruction during an investigation can mean manually searching several sources.

My contribution

Built a proof-of-concept assistant using retrieval-augmented generation to retrieve contextual guidance from SOPs, runbooks, and internal-style operational documentation.

Implementation

The documented approach uses RAG and LlamaIndex to connect natural-language questions with relevant operational content. The use cases include workflow instructions, troubleshooting procedures, and escalation guidance.

Documented outcome

This is documented as an exploration of knowledge retrieval, prompt optimization, and contextual responses. It is not presented as a deployed enterprise assistant or as evidence of measured answer accuracy.

Scope & evidence

The source material does not identify an embedding model, vector store, chunking configuration, evaluation results, or access-control implementation. No private operational documents are exposed on this website.

A useful next conversation

A future public demonstration should use synthetic documents and measure grounded answers, retrieval relevance, and appropriate abstention.
